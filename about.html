Meet Safar Wheels

Safar means journey. We help Lahore travel in comfort, for weddings, family visits, business and tours.

Our story

Safar Wheels is a family-run rental company in Lahore. We started with two small cars and a promise: clear prices, clean vehicles and polite drivers.

Today we look after hatchbacks for the city, sedans for business, SUVs for the north, vans for families, luxury cars for weddings and buses for big groups. Whatever your trip, we want you to arrive happy.
